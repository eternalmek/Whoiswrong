# WhoIsWrong organic launch experiment

Status: prepared copy, not published. Do not send paid traffic until a real test-mode checkout, account unlock, renewal, and cancellation have been verified against the deployed backend.

## Positioning

**Settle your pettiest argument. Let an AI judge pick a side.**

Supporting copy: Two opinions. One entertaining verdict. Try the free AI judge, then share the result with your group chat.

Keep the entertainment framing visible. AI characters are simulations, not endorsements or statements by the people depicted. Never present an invented verdict as an actual customer's experience. Use fictional examples rather than private messages or disputes from identifiable people.

## First experiment

Publish one short screen recording per day for 14 days through the verified brand account once publishing access exists. Use the free AI judge and capture the actual verdict. Do not write a favorable verdict into a mockup and claim it came from the app.

Structure: show the disagreement immediately; display both sides; reveal the real app verdict; ask viewers to choose A or B. Keep the website name visible and readable. Make a video only after the free verdict flow works.

Record the published post URL and actual publishing time. UTM parameters alone do not measure conversions: verify analytics captures the landing source before relying on attribution.

## Scorecard

Track per post: views, profile visits, website visits, completed verdicts, shares, signups, checkout starts, confirmed paid purchases, refunds, collected revenue, and variable costs. Leave unavailable values blank, not zero. Never infer revenue from button clicks or local browser unlocks.

The first milestones are ten completed free verdicts by real visitors, ten voluntary shares, then the first verified paying customer. These are validation goals, not forecasts or evidence of product-market fit.

After seven posts, compare which disagreements bring completed verdicts and shares, not just views. Continue the strongest theme in the second week. If visitors arrive but verdicts fail, fix the product. If verdicts work but nobody shares, improve the result card and sharing flow. If users share but do not buy, test a clearer paid benefit before changing prices.

Do not start paid advertising or promise earnings from this experiment. Review recurring profit, refunds, retention, and support workload over multiple months before treating the website as replacement employment income.
